Annual Leave Policy

1. Purpose
This policy outlines the annual leave entitlement for all employees of the company.

2. Entitlement
Full-time employees are entitled to 25 days of annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated entitlement based on their contracted hours.

3. Leave Year
The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December. Unused leave may be carried over to the next year up to a maximum of 5 days.

4. Requesting Leave
Employees must submit leave requests through SAP SuccessFactors at least 5 working days in advance. Approval is subject to business needs and manager discretion.

5. Public Holidays
Public holidays are in addition to the annual leave entitlement. Employees working on a public holiday will receive a day in lieu.

6. Leave During Notice Period
Annual leave may not normally be taken during a notice period without manager approval.
