Bereavement Leave Policy

1. Purpose
This policy provides guidance on compassionate and bereavement leave for employees dealing with the death of a family member or close friend.

2. Entitlement
Employees are entitled to up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave upon the death of an immediate family member. Immediate family includes spouse or partner, children, parents, siblings, and grandparents.

For the death of an extended family member (e.g., aunt, uncle, cousin), employees are entitled to 1 day of paid bereavement leave.

3. Requesting Bereavement Leave
Employees should notify their line manager as soon as possible. Formal requests should be submitted through HR as soon as practicable. Documentation may be requested in some circumstances.

4. Additional Leave
In exceptional circumstances, employees may request additional unpaid leave. This is at the discretion of the line manager and HR.

5. Returning to Work
Managers are encouraged to have a supportive return-to-work conversation with employees returning from bereavement leave.
