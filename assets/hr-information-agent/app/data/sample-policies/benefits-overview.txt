Employee Benefits Overview

1. Health and Wellbeing
All employees have access to the company health insurance plan from their first day of employment. The plan covers GP visits, specialist consultations, hospital stays, and dental and vision care. Employees may add dependants to their plan at their own cost.

2. Pension and Retirement
The company operates a defined contribution pension scheme. The company matches employee contributions up to 5% of gross salary. Employees are automatically enrolled after 3 months of employment.

3. Life Assurance
All employees are covered by a life assurance policy equal to 4 times their annual salary. This is provided at no cost to the employee.

4. Employee Assistance Programme (EAP)
The company provides a confidential Employee Assistance Programme offering counselling, financial advice, and legal support. This service is available 24/7 to all employees and their immediate family members.

5. Learning and Development
Employees are entitled to an annual learning and development budget of 1,500 EUR for approved courses, certifications, and conferences.

6. Flexible Working
Employees may apply for flexible working arrangements including remote work, compressed hours, or part-time schedules after completing their probationary period.

7. Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 26 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers are entitled to 4 weeks of paid parental leave. Employees must give 8 weeks notice of their intention to take parental leave.
